# Fletchery
A local app that walks writers through building a Story Bible as plain Markdown and images, for AI-assisted writing.

## Sections

Each story type has its own set of sections, and every one also gets Notes and media. Premise is built so far. The rest are planned.

- **Prose fiction:** Premise, Characters, Relationships, World and settings, Timeline and events, Plot and structure, Themes, motifs, and symbols, Voice and style, and Series continuity (for a series).
- **Screenplay:** Logline and premise, Characters, Relationships, World and settings, Timeline and events, Structure, Themes, motifs, and symbols, Voice and format, and Series continuity (for a series).
- **Game narrative:** Premise, Characters, Relationships, World and settings, Factions, Timeline and events, Plot and structure, Quests, Choices and branching, Narrative and mechanics, Items and glossary, Themes, motifs, and symbols, Voice and dialogue conventions, and Series continuity (for sequels).

Each entry can be filled at three depths: a Seed (one line), a Sketch (one paragraph), or a Sheet (the full version). For the Premise, the Sheet is about a page in one box. For entries like characters, it will be the full template.
